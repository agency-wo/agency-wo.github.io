What we do

Five ways to be easier to find.

  • SEO and local search

    Getting found on Google, both in the results and on the map. On-page work on the site itself, off-page work everywhere else, and your Google Business Profile set up properly.

  • AI search

    When somebody asks ChatGPT or Gemini for a shop like yours, it names two or three. This is the work of being one of them.

  • Websites

    Fast, clear sites that a machine can read and a customer can buy from. Usually in English, Italian and Albanian.

  • Meta ads

    Facebook and Instagram, for customers this week while the slower work builds up. A flat fee, never a percentage of your budget.

  • Custom software

    The tool your business is missing. Update your own site from your phone, connect the till to the website, or have the thing built that does not exist.
