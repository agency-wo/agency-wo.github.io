Proof

Three months ago, Google had never heard of this shop.

Iglisi Watch repairs watches and sells them, on Rruga Aleksander Goga in Durres. In May there was no website at all, so the starting number really is zero. Everything on this chart came from search, not from an ad budget.

  • 560clicks from Google
  • 57.6ktimes shown
  • 8.4average position
  • 1%click rate

Three months, 12 May to 9 August 2026. Search Console reports clicks, which is not the same as people.

Ads stop the day you stop paying for them. This does not: the shop was put on the map once, and search has been sending people ever since.

Position 8.4 is the bottom of the first page and a 1% click rate is about what the bottom of the first page pays. Moving that up is the next job, and it is where the rest of the growth is.

Search for watch repair in Durres. Then search for a watch shop in Durres. Then ask ChatGPT both questions, and see whose name keeps coming back.

Taken August 2026. Rankings move, so the chart will look different by the time you read this.
