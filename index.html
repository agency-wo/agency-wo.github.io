Questions

Before you send anything

What does this cost?

There is no price list. A shop with 4 pages and a shop with 400 are not the same job. The number comes out of the audit.

How soon does it start working?

Two clocks, running at different speeds. A map listing can move inside a month. Ordinary search takes far longer, which is why we gave it a whole page rather than a line here.

What happens after we send the form?

You get a written document, not an invitation to a meeting. It says what we would change and in what order. If the honest answer is that your money is better spent elsewhere, it says that instead.

Do we have to replace our website?

Usually not. Most sites need repairs rather than a rebuild, and a rebuild nobody needed throws away whatever ranking the old one had earned.

What do you not do?

Print, video production, and posting to social media on a schedule. We also do not run campaigns on budgets that cannot carry them. Hearing that now costs both of us less than hearing it in month 3.
