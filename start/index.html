Questions worth asking

How long have you been doing this?

minarank studio is new. The work is public rather than described: there is a page for every business we have built for, and for the first of them the whole Search Console export, weak numbers included. That is the part you can check, which is more than a founding date tells you.

What happens to my site if we stop working together?

You keep all of it. The domain, the code, the hosting and every account are in your name from day one, not ours. There is no handover to negotiate, because nothing was ever held on your behalf.

Who actually does the work?
