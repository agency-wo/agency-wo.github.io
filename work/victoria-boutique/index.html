Victoria Boutique

Durres, Albania. Fashion. victoriaboutique.org

Where this started

A boutique that brings Greek labels into Albania, changing stock with the season. The clothes were the whole business and none of them were online.

Anything built here had to survive the owner adding pieces every week without calling us, or it would go stale by the second month.

What we built

  • A site built around the clothes and the shop itself, photographed, rather than around stock imagery.
  • Albanian, English and Italian, with a language switch that works even with JavaScript turned off.
  • A panel where she adds, edits and removes pieces from her phone. No content system to license, no monthly fee, nobody to call.

Who runs the website now

She does. The shop updates its own site, which means the site keeps up with the stock instead of drifting a season behind it.

This is also the build where a one-off became something we could hand to the next client.
