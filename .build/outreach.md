# Outreach, ready to send

`citations.md` says what to do and in what order. This says **what to write**, because the gap
between those two is where the task has sat since launch.

Every message here is short enough to send on WhatsApp, which is where these four clients
actually reply. Send them one at a time and on different days: four identical requests arriving
in one afternoon reads as a campaign, and a campaign is easier to ignore than a person.

---

## 1. Review requests

The three Durres clients get Albanian. ProAffy gets English. **Skip whichever one you have
already asked** rather than sending twice.

Each message names the specific job. That is not politeness, it is the difference between a
review that says "good service" and one that says what you did, and only the second is worth
having when somebody is deciding between you and an agency in Tirana.

> para se të na shkruajnë. Mjafton një rresht për faqen dhe për atë që ndryshoi në kërkim.

### ProAffy, in English

> Hi, hope things are going well. One small favour if you have a minute: a Google review for the
> studio would help us a lot. We are new and reviews are how people decide whether to write to
> us. A line about the ads work is plenty.
> Here is the link: [PASTE THE REVIEW LINK]

### The review link

Get it from the Business Profile, the short share link made for exactly this. Paste it into all
four messages before sending any of them. Asking somebody to search for you and find the button
loses most people, and the ones you lose are the ones who meant to.

### If nothing arrives after a week

Send once, then stop. Two follow-ups on a favour costs more than the review is worth.

> Përshëndetje, po e ngre sërish vetëm një herë në rast se humbi mes mesazheve. Nëse nuk të
> shkon, s'ka problem fare.

## 2. ProAffy, the missing link

Three of the four client sites link back and are followable. ProAffy is the one that does not,
and it has been outstanding since launch. It is worth more than any directory on the list because
it is a real site in the same trade.

> Hi, one more small thing. Would you mind adding a credit line in the footer linking to
> minarankstudio.com? Something like "Site by minarank studio" is perfect. It helps us more than
> it probably looks, and it is the normal thing for a studio credit.

If they say yes and nothing happens for a fortnight, offer to make the change yourself. You built
the site; the edit is a minute and asking a busy person to do a minute of work is often what
stalls it.

## 3. Directories

`citations.md` holds the exact strings. **Paste from that file and change nothing**, including
`Durres` without the diaeresis, which is deliberate and explained there.

The order matters and it is not alphabetical:

| # | Where | Why it is in this position |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bing Places | One-click import from the Google profile. Bing feeds ChatGPT Search, so this is the cheapest AI visibility on the list. |
| 2 | Albanian directories | Free, local, and they build the entity before Google will trust a new domain on its own. |
| 3 | Sortlist / Clutch / GoodFirms | Agency directories. Slower forms, real referral traffic, and they carry weight for "agency" searches. |
| 4 | Instagram and Facebook bios | The links do not pass authority, but they are crawled fast and they are consistent NAP. |

Do one sitting per row rather than one per site. The forms all ask for the same six fields and
you will be faster on the fourth than the first.

## The thing worth remembering

None of this is marketing. It is four short messages and about an hour of forms, and between them
they are worth more right now than any page anybody could write, because the domain is nine days
old and reviews and links are the two signals that do not need time to accumulate on their own.
